puterminator

privacy and disclaimer

puterminator is a uinaf side project. put.io doesn’t make, run or endorse it.

# what it keeps

  • Your put.io username, avatar and put.io token.
  • A snapshot of your file list: names, sizes, dates, checksums and whether each file was played.
  • Your chats and plans.
  • All of it lives in a Cloudflare database that only your account can reach.
  • This month’s credit use, under a random id with no name on it, in a shared total that keeps everyone’s free credits within budget.

# where it goes

  • Your messages and the file names they need go to Claude, under Anthropic’s commercial terms.
  • When you ask for a subtitle translation, the subtitle text goes to Claude too.
  • Videos stream from put.io straight to your browser.
  • No ads, no trackers. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies.

# what it changes

  • Nothing changes until you apply a plan. The model can propose a plan but can’t apply one.
  • Trashed files go to your put.io trash. Translated subtitles are uploaded next to their video. Undo reverses a plan you applied.
  • If trash is off in your put.io settings, puterminator won’t trash anything.
  • puterminator comes as is, with no warranty. The model can be wrong about a file, so read every plan before you apply it.

# your data

  • Sign out ends every session and deletes your put.io token.
  • forget me in the account menu deletes your chats, plans, file list snapshot, put.io token, username and avatar, then signs you out everywhere. It keeps only this month’s credit use, so deleting doesn’t reset your credits. Your put.io files stay as they are.
  • To cut access for good, revoke puterminator in your put.io account settings.
