puterminator

privacy and disclaimer

puterminator is a side project by uinaf. put.io doesn’t make, run or endorse it.

# what it reads

  • Your put.io file list under the folder you pick: names, sizes, types, dates, checksums, and whether a file was ever played.
  • Your put.io username and avatar, to show who is signed in.

# where it goes

  • The file list, your chat and your plans stay in storage on Cloudflare that belongs to your put.io account alone.
  • To answer you, your messages and the file names they need go to Claude through Anthropic’s API, under Anthropic’s commercial terms.
  • Video previews stream from put.io straight to your browser.
  • No ads and no third-party trackers. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies.

# what it changes

  • Renames, moves and trash run only when you apply a plan in the browser. The model proposes plans; it can't apply them.
  • Trash goes to your put.io trash, where you can restore it. Undo reverses what a plan did.
  • If trash is off in your put.io settings, puterminator refuses to trash anything, since that would delete for good.

# no warranty

puterminator comes as is, with no warranty of any kind. The model can be wrong about what a file is. Read every plan before you apply it: you are responsible for the plans you apply.

# your data

  • Sign out to end every session and drop your put.io token.
  • To remove access for good, revoke puterminator in your put.io account settings.
  • To delete everything puterminator keeps for you, or to ask anything, email dev@uinaf.dev.
